## What is CallFrame?

[CallFrame](https://callframehq.com) is an AI-powered call preparation tool built for Sales Development Representatives. It automatically researches prospects and generates contextual briefings before every call — so reps spend less time Googling and more time selling.

## Why I Built It

After years of watching sales and applications teams prepare for customer conversations manually — pulling up LinkedIn, scanning CRM notes, reviewing past interactions — I saw an opportunity to automate the entire prep workflow with AI.

## How It Works

CallFrame sits in the SDR's workflow and does the research legwork automatically. Before each call, reps get a briefing with the context they need to have a great conversation — prospect background, company intel, relevant talking points, and potential pain points.
